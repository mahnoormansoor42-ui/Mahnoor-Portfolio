Background & Expertise

About the Designer

👩‍🏫

Mahnoor Mansoor

Creator of Bright Sprout Studio • Speech-Language Pathologist

I am Mahnoor Mansoor, the creator behind Bright Sprout Studio. I design purposeful, high-engagement digital workbooks and printable activities crafted specifically for preschool and early-learning children.

My professional foundation is rooted in Speech-Language Pathology, with over 9 years of dedicated clinical experience as a Speech Therapist. Working directly with diverse early learners has provided me with deep insight into developmental milestones, cognitive readiness, and how children best absorb language and concepts.

This clinical expertise directly informs every resource I create. Rather than generic worksheets, each Bright Sprout Studio printable is intentionally structured to support early communication, fine-motor coordination, creative expression, confidence, and cognitive problem-solving through playful, low-frustration design.
