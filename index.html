The Heart Behind Bright Sprout

Hi! I’m Mahnoor Mansoor

Creator of Bright Sprout Studio • Speech-Language Pathologist

As a practicing Speech Therapist for more than 9 years, I have worked with hundreds of children struggling with speech articulation, pencil grip, fine motor frustration, and expressive communication.

I founded Bright Sprout Studio with one core mission: to replace overwhelming, rigid worksheets with joyful, progressive printables that children actually love doing. Every activity page is intentionally engineered so that drawing, tracing, and phonics reinforce one another naturally.
